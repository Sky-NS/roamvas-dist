Delete your Roamvas account

This page explains how to delete your account in Roamvas, the AI travel-planning app, and exactly what happens to your data.

In the app — takes a minute

  1. Open Roamvas and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Open More → Delete account.
  4. Enter your password to confirm.

Deletion happens immediately. It cannot be undone.

By email — if you cannot sign in

Write to support@roamvas.app from the email address linked to your account, with the subject Account deletion request, and include your username.

We reply within 30 days, usually much sooner. We may ask you to confirm your identity if the request does not come from the address on the account.

What is deleted

Everything below is erased permanently and immediately:

  • Your account, username and email address
  • Your password hash and all active sessions on every device
  • All travel plans that nobody has saved to their favourites — including the photos attached to them
  • Your views, likes, dislikes and favourites in the public feed
  • Moderation records and any warnings on the account
  • Promo codes you redeemed

What is kept, and why

One exception. A plan you published that another user has already saved to their favourites is not deleted — deleting it would remove it from their account too, and that is not yours to take away.

Such a plan is removed from the public feed and stays visible only to the people who saved it. Your account is unlinked from it: your name is no longer shown or stored alongside it.

If you want those removed as well, unpublish or delete the plans before deleting your account, or ask us at support@roamvas.app.

Retention period

There is no retention period for the data listed above under “What is deleted” — it is removed at the moment of deletion, not queued or frozen. Routine infrastructure backups may hold residual copies for up to 30 days, after which they are overwritten.

We keep no billing records: subscriptions are handled entirely by Apple and Google, who never pass payment details to us.

Data stored only on your device

Your profile picture, PIN and biometric setting never leave your device — they are not on our servers, so there is nothing for us to delete. They are removed when you delete the account, sign out, or uninstall the app.
